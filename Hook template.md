
# 🪝 Hook Templates
> [!IMPORTANT]
> These are templates, not finished hooks. Replace the bracketed text with specifics related to you, your audience, and your topics. The more specific you make them, the better they will perform.
> 
## 🧐 Curiosity Hooks
 * After two years of doing **[common practice]**, I figured out why most **[audience]** never **[outcome]**.
 * The way most **[audience]** approach **[topic]** is the reason they stay stuck at **[problem]**.
 * A client told me something last week about **[topic]** that I had never considered before.
 * There is a reason your **[thing]** keeps **[problem]**, and it has nothing to do with **[common assumption]**.
 * I used to believe **[common belief]**. Then **[specific event]** happened, and I see this completely differently now.
## 🚫 Contrarian Hooks
 * Everyone says you need **[common advice]** to succeed at **[outcome]**. I have built **[proof]** doing the opposite.
 * The advice to **[common practice]** is the worst thing you can give a new **[audience]**.
 * Most of the people teaching **[topic]** online have never actually done it themselves.
 * I disagree with almost every piece of **[topic]** advice circulating on LinkedIn right now.
 * You do not need **[common requirement]** to **[outcome]**. I built **[specific proof]** without any of it.
## 📖 Story Hooks
 * Three years ago I was **[low point]**. Today I run **[contrast outcome]**. Here is what changed in between.
 * A founder I coached last month came to me with **[problem]**. Within **[time period]** they had **[outcome]**. Here is how.
 * The first time I tried **[thing]**, I lost **[specific loss]**. The second time taught me something I now share with every client.
 * Yesterday someone asked me how I went from **[old situation]** to **[current situation]**. The answer goes back to one moment in **[year]**.
 * I almost gave up on **[thing]** in **[year]**. Then **[specific person]** said something to me that I have never forgotten.
## 🔢 Listicle Hooks
 * Five things I wish someone had told me before I started **[topic]**.
 * Three habits that quietly killed my **[outcome]** for the first two years.
 * Seven questions I ask every **[audience]** before agreeing to work with them.
 * Four signs you are ready to **[next step]**, and one sign you are not.
 * The six pieces of advice I would give my younger self about building **[thing]**.
## ✨ Transformation Hooks
 * Six months ago I could not **[skill]**. Now I **[outcome]**. The shift came from one specific change.
 * My **[metric]** went from **[low number]** to **[high number]** in **[time period]**. Here is what actually moved the needle.
 * I went from **[previous situation]** to **[current situation]** by doing the unglamorous work nobody talks about.
 * The version of me from two years ago would not recognise the business I run today.
 * My first **[thing]** generated **[low outcome]**. My latest **[thing]** generated **[high outcome]**. The difference is one skill.
## ❓ Question Hooks
 * What separates the **[audience]** who **[succeed]** from the ones who stay stuck for years?
 * Why do so many **[audience]** confuse being busy with making real progress?
 * When was the last time your **[thing]** actually moved the needle on your business?
 * What would your **[thing]** look like if you stopped chasing **[common practice]** tomorrow?
 * Where will you be in twelve months if nothing about your current approach changes?
