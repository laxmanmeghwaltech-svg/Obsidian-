# 15 Websites That Make Learning Actually Fun (+1 Bonus)

> [!summary]
> 16 interactive, game-style sites that replace passive reading with hands-on practice across geography, code, science, music, languages, and first aid.
> **Source video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3M_nmmefY6c)

---

## 🗺️ Geography & History

### 1. Seterra `[00:00:20]`
* **Link:** [seterra.com](https://www.seterra.com/)
* **Domain:** World geography
* **How it works:** Map quizzes with instant visual feedback.
* **Coverage:** Countries, capitals, flags, states, and regions.
* **Why it works:** Turns borders into recall drills instead of memorization.

### 2. GeaCron `[00:01:16]`
* **Link:** [geacron.com](https://geacron.com/)
* **Domain:** World history
* **How it works:** Interactive timeline atlas across eras.
* **Why it works:** You scrub through centuries and watch empires rise, collapse, and shift borders on the map.

---

## 💻 Tech, Code & Systems

### 3. SQLBolt `[00:03:36]`
* **Link:** [sqlbolt.com](https://sqlbolt.com/)
* **Domain:** SQL and relational databases
* **How it works:** In-browser interpreter.
* **Why it works:** Short syntax lessons, then live queries against sample data right away.

### 4. Flexbox Froggy `[00:04:06]`
* **Link:** [flexboxfroggy.com](https://flexboxfroggy.com/)
* **Domain:** CSS
* **How it works:** Layout puzzle where CSS properties move frogs onto lily pads.
* **Why it works:** You see `justify-content`, `align-items`, and `flex-direction` change the screen as you type.

### 5. NandGame `[00:05:20]`
* **Link:** [nandgame.com](https://nandgame.com/)
* **Domain:** Computer architecture and digital logic
* **How it works:** Hardware-building puzzle inspired by *From Nand to Tetris*.
* **Why it works:** Starts from a single NAND gate and builds up to an ALU and a processor.

### 6. Hello Morse `[00:04:56]`
* **Link:** [experiments.withgoogle.com/collection/morse](https://experiments.withgoogle.com/collection/morse)
* **Domain:** Morse code
* **How it works:** Audio and visual pattern games.
* **Why it works:** Dots and dashes become sound and rhythm cues you can recognize.

---

## 🔬 Science, Math & Anatomy

### 7. Mathigon `[00:03:12]`
* **Link:** [mathigon.org](https://mathigon.org/)
* **Domain:** Mathematics
* **How it works:** "Textbook of the future" with visual things to play with.
* **Coverage:** Fractals, polyhedra, graph theory, geometry, probability.
* **Why it works:** You build intuition by manipulating shapes, not by memorizing formulas.

### 8. UCAR Thunderstorm Simulator `[00:01:46]`
* **Link:** [scied.ucar.edu](https://scied.ucar.edu/interactive/make-thunderstorm)
* **Domain:** Meteorology
* **How it works:** You adjust temperature, humidity, and atmospheric stability.
* **Why it works:** Shows cause and effect in how storms form.

### 9. Clara Atlas `[00:04:32]`
* **Link:** [clara.io](https://clara.io/)
* **Domain:** Human anatomy
* **How it works:** 3D organ and skeleton viewer.
* **Why it works:** Rotate, zoom, and peel back layers to see how body parts fit together.

---

## 🧠 Cognition, Logic & Music

### 10. The Evolution of Trust `[00:05:48]`
* **Link:** [ncase.me/trust](https://ncase.me/trust/)
* **Domain:** Game theory
* **Author:** Nicky Case
* **How it works:** Simulation of the repeated Prisoner's Dilemma.
* **Why it works:** Shows how cooperation, cheating, and miscommunication change trust over generations.

### 11. Teoria `[00:02:08]`
* **Link:** [teoria.com](https://teoria.com/)
* **Domain:** Music theory and ear training
* **How it works:** Ear-training and harmony drills.
* **Coverage:** Intervals, chords, scale modes, rhythm dictation.

### 12. Zerdly Memory `[00:02:32]`
* **Link:** [zerdly.com](https://www.zerdly.com/)
* **Domain:** Working memory
* **How it works:** Adaptive drills (digit spans, spatial grids, sequences).

### 13. Quick, Draw! `[00:06:16]`
* **Link:** [quickdraw.withgoogle.com](https://quickdraw.withgoogle.com/)
* **Domain:** Machine learning
* **How it works:** You sketch, and Google's neural network guesses in real time.
* **Why it works:** Shows how a vision model classifies patterns learned from huge datasets.

---

## 🌐 Languages, Security & Life Skills

### 14. Digital Dialects `[00:00:51]`
* **Link:** [digitaldialects.com](https://www.digitaldialects.com/)
* **Domain:** Languages
* **How it works:** Light matching and listening games for basic vocabulary in dozens of languages.

### 15. Jigsaw Phishing Quiz `[00:02:46]`
* **Link:** [phishingquiz.withgoogle.com](https://phishingquiz.withgoogle.com/)
* **Domain:** Cybersecurity
* **How it works:** You inspect real-looking messages and spot the fake.
* **Why it works:** Trains you to catch spoofed links, odd URLs, and header red flags.

---

### 🎁 Bonus: Lifesaver `[00:06:44]`
* **Link:** [lifesaver.org.uk](https://lifesaver.org.uk/)
* **Domain:** First aid and CPR
* **Organization:** Resuscitation Council UK
* **How it works:** Branching video scenarios where you make decisions in real time.
* **Why it works:** Puts you under emergency pressure (choking, cardiac arrest) so the steps become automatic.
