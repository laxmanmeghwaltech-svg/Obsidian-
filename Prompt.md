POST PRODUCTION CHAIN — Reusable Template
# One input. Eight outputs. Works for any post topic.

## INPUTS (you should decide according to on what you are going to work)

TOPIC: [What is this post about?]
MAIN CLAIM: [The one specific, defensible point you're making]
STAT: [One real number that backs the claim]
STORY: [One personal experience of 2-3 lines that proves it]
CONTRARIAN ANGLE: [The take that disagrees with the obvious consensus]
VOICE PROFILE: [Paste your saved voice profile, or reference voice.md]
Everything type of file is available in project knowledge use them for writing best and for me  
---

## OUTPUT 1 — VOICE PROFILE SNAPSHOT
Analyze the voice profile provided. Summarize the top 5 sentence patterns,
the 3 recurring themes, and the 3 things this voice never does.
Output as a quick-reference card.

---

## OUTPUT 2 — 10 HOOKS
Write 10 scroll-stopping hooks for [TOPIC].
Rules: 6-7 words max. Specific over clever. First-person or data-led.
No rhetorical questions. No generic "Here's what I learned" openers.

---

## OUTPUT 3 — WINNING HOOK + OUTLINE
Pick the strongest hook from Output 2. Explain why in one sentence.
Then outline the post: Hook / Re-hook / 3 beats (stat, story, contrarian) /
Transition line / Closing challenge. No full sentences yet — just the skeleton.

---

## OUTPUT 4 — BEAT DEVELOPMENT
Expand the 3 beats:
Beat 1 (stat): [STAT] with context. What does the number mean in practice?
Beat 2 (story): [STORY] told in 4-5 short lines. First person. Specific detail.
Beat 3 (contrarian): Use [CONTRARIAN ANGLE]. State the obvious consensus.
Then disagree with it. Show why the obvious is wrong in this context.

---

## OUTPUT 5 — FULL DRAFT (LinkedIn formatted)
Write the full post using the voice profile and beats.
Rules: Sentences under 55 characters. Blank line between beats.
First 2 lines carry the whole post. Re-hook on line 2.
No filler transitions. No inspirational summaries at the end.
Closing line = direct question to the reader.
Output inside a code block.

---

## OUTPUT 6 — AI TELL AUDIT + CLEAN VERSION
Scan Output 5 for: rhetorical questions, em dashes, adverbs,
sloganeering closers, hedging words, announced transitions
("Here's the contrarian part"), and promotional phrasing.
List each flag in one line. Then rewrite the post with all tells removed.
Output the cleaned version in a code block.

---

## OUTPUT 7 — COUNCIL PRESSURE TEST
Run the final cleaned post through the LLM Council.
Frame the question as: "Is this post strong enough to stop a scroll,
hold a read, and earn a comment from [target audience]?
What would make it fail, and what would make it land harder?"
Produce the full council verdict: where advisors agree,
where they clash, blind spots caught, and one concrete improvement.
