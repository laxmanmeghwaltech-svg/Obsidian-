
# 🚀 LinkedIn Carousel Script Strategy
Use this framework to create high-performing LinkedIn carousels based on the strategies of Lara Acosta.
## 📝 Step 1: The Script Structure
**The Hook (Slide 1): Attention**
 * **Source Rule:** The hook must be 8 words or fewer to avoid being cut off on mobile feeds.
 * **Source Rule:** Focus on a specific outcome or number (Monetizable Expertise).
 * **Script Template:**
   * **Headline:** "How I [Specific Achievement] in [Timeframe]." (Max 8 words).
   * **Sub-headline:** "A breakdown of my exact strategy."
**The Re-Hook & Story (Slide 2): Retention**
 * **Source Rule:** The re-hook is your second chance to keep the reader.
 * **Source Rule:** Use specific metrics or numbers to build trust.
 * **Source Rule:** Use the "How I" angle rather than "How to"; personal experience builds more trust than generic advice.
 * **Script Template:**
   * **Text:** "Last night I [Action/Achievement]. I started [Timeframe] ago. Here is exactly how I did it...".
   * **Visual Note:** This slide must include a placeholder for an **authentic photo** (e.g., you working or a client call). A story without a photo loses impact.
**The Lesson (Slide 3): Transition**
 * **Source Rule:** Transition from your story to the core value. Explain why this matters to the reader.
 * **Script Template:**
   * **Text:** "Most people think [Common Myth]. But I discovered [Your Insight]. Here are the [Number] steps that changed everything:"
**Actionable Advice (Slides 4-7): The "Meat"**
 * **Source Rule:** Give it all away. Do not gatekeep; share your "secret sauce" or blueprints.
 * **Source Rule:** Use the "F-Shape Pattern" with varied sentence lengths (one line, space, one line, space) to make it skimmable.
 * **Script Template (Repeat for each step):**
   * **Header:** Step [X]: [Action Name].
   * **Body:** * "Do this specific thing."
     * (Space)
     * "Because it leads to this result."
     * (Space)
     * "Avoid this common mistake."
**The "You" (Slide 8): Conclusion**
 * **Source Rule:** End with a "feel-good" quote or cliché to leave the reader feeling positive.
 * **Source Rule:** Use a specific Call to Action (CTA) or "Call to Engage" to boost comments and drive the algorithm in the first 30 minutes.
 * **Script Template:**
   * **Quote:** "[Timeframe] ago I never thought I'd be here. The long nights were worth it."
   * **CTA:** "Repost this if you found it helpful. Comment '[Keyword]' for my full SOP."
## 🎨 Step 2: Design Instructions ("Grey Minimal" Theme)
Use these specific constraints to ensure reliability and high engagement:
 1. **The F-Shape Layout:**
   * **Instruction:** Arrange text in an F-shape pattern, left-aligned. Alternate between short lines and empty space. Avoid block paragraphs to keep retention high.
   * **Theme Application:** On a grey minimal background, this use of whitespace looks premium and prevents a "wall of text."
 2. **Visual Hierarchy:**
   * **Instruction:** The Hook (Slide 1) must be big and bold. Slides 2-8 should use a clean, sans-serif font with plenty of negative space.
   * **Source Insight:** Treat content like short-form video; it must be "bingeable."
 3. **Authenticity over Aesthetics:**
   * **Instruction:** On Slide 2, use a real photo of yourself instead of an illustration.
   * **Source Insight:** Authentic photos outperform polished stock images because they prove the story is real.
 4. **Trend Jacking (Optional Add-on):**
   * **Instruction:** Reference a current event or high-authority figure to leverage the "Halo Effect."
## ✅ Summary Checklist for Reliability

 * [ ] Is the text formatted in the F-Shape (line, space, line)?
 * [ ] Did you include a "Feel Good" quote at the end?
 * [ ] Are you engaging heavily for the first 30 minutes after posting?
